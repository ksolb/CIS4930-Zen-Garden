# Japanese Zen Garden Moodboard

# Visual Direction

The garden should feel like a peaceful, modern Japanese Zen garden. The environment will combine 
traditional Japanese garden elements with a polished and realistic visual style.

The scene should emphasize natural materials, open space, soft lighting, and a calm atmosphere.

## Mood Descriptions

- Serene
- Meditative
- Natural
- Weathered
- Minimal

## Color Palette

- Moss Green: Moss and ground foliage: `#596B45`
- Dark Green: Trees and background vegetation: `#263D2A`
- Gray: Rocks, paths, and lanterns: `#74746F`
- Light Tan: Raked sand and gravel: `#D3C29C`
- Dark Brown: Bridges, fences, and structures: `#4A3326`
- Blue: Ponds and reflections: `#6F9299`
- Red: Small architectural accents: `#873F36`

## Lighting Direction

- Time of day: Early morning
- Key-light direction: Low-angle sunlight entering from one side of the garden
-  Color temperature: Soft and slightly warm
- Shadow style: Soft-edged shadows filtered through trees
- *Atmosphere: Light mist or subtle atmospheric haze may be used to create depth
- Avoid: Harsh midday lighting, deep black shadows, or overly dramatic cinematic contrast

## Material Direction

### Stone

Stone should appear slightly weathered, uneven, and naturally aged. Some stones may have light moss growth, but they should not look abandoned or ruined.

### Moss

Moss should be soft, dense, and muted green. It should appear around stones, trees, lanterns, and damp shaded areas.

### Water

Water should be calm and reflective with subtle movement. Reflections should support the peaceful atmosphere rather than appearing highly animated.

### Wood

Wood should be dark, natural, and lightly weathered. Structures should not look newly painted or artificially glossy.

### Sand and Gravel

Sand should use warm neutral colors. Raked patterns should be visible but clean, simple, and not overly complex.

### Foliage

Foliage should mainly use natural greens, with limited use of cherry blossoms or red maple leaves as visual accents.


## Anti-References

The garden should not resemble:

- A neon or super bright atmosphere
- An overwhelming amount of audio and moving visuals
- An overly crowded jungle with a lot of moving parts
